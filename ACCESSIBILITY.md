# Accessibility

The aim is to make this port easier to play and its launcher easier to use. Accessibility
improvements and reports are welcome, including small fixes to controls, text and navigation.

## Current options

The port runs on Linux, Steam Deck and Windows. It supports controllers and keyboard and mouse
input, with rebindable controls and an in-game controls overlay. The launcher offers light and
dark themes. See [Configuration](README.md#configuration) for the available settings.

## Known limits

- In-game prompts still show controller buttons when playing with a keyboard and mouse.
- Screen-reader support and full keyboard navigation in the launcher have not been verified.
- The game retains much of the original interface. Text scaling, contrast and colour accessibility
  have not been fully assessed.

## Reporting a barrier

Use the [accessibility report template](https://github.com/YesterMester/TheSimpsonsGameRecomp/issues/new?template=accessibility_report.md).
Describe the task you were trying to complete, where you got stuck, your platform and version,
and any input device or assistive software involved. A description is enough; screenshots and
videos are optional. You do not need to share a diagnosis or medical information.
